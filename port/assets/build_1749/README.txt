halo1749.exe plays Halo's build 01.08.15.1749 (15 August 2001) from this
folder. Nothing of that build comes with OpenCE: copy your own dump of it here.

    1749\maps\ui.map          the build's menus
    1749\maps\*.map           its levels (a10.map, b30.map, bloodgulch.map...)
    1749\default.xbe           optional: the build's executable
    1749\bink\                optional: its videos

halo1749.exe keeps its own settings (1749\config.toml) and saves
(1749\savegame), apart from halo.exe's. It plays stock: the Xbox menus out of
1749's ui.map, at 30 frames a second, with OpenCE's additions turned off.
Its maps will not play in halo.exe, nor the retail maps in halo1749.exe.
